# Short Interview Insert

## Format

```markdown
# [Insert title]

- Duration: [10–20 seconds or supplied duration]
- Aspect ratio: [ratio]
- Visual style: [style]
- Insert point: [where it enters the interview]

## Timed script
- [00:00–00:04] Visual: [action] | Dialogue/Narration: [line or none]
- [00:04–00:09] Visual: [action] | Dialogue/Narration: [line or none]
- [00:09–00:15] Visual: [action] | Transition: [return to real footage]

## Continuity
- [character, clothing, environment, palette, prop invariants]

## Negative constraints
- [unsupported text, objects, settings, or identity changes]
```

Use no more than three or four readable beats for a 10–20 second insert. Let an overlong closing thought continue after returning to the interview.

## Example

Input: `A child who usually waits silently joins a queue and asks for a turn. Make a 12-second insert that returns to the parent's interview.`

Expected sequence: establish the queue → show the child step forward and ask → show the small response → cut back to the parent as the reflection continues.
