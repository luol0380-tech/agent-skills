# Character Continuity Sheet

## Format

```markdown
# [Character code] — Continuity lock

## Identity
- Age impression: [description]
- Face and expression: [description]
- Hair and glasses: [description]
- Height relationship: [description]

## Clothing and accessories
- Top: [details]
- Bottom: [details]
- Shoes: [details]
- Stable accessories: [details]

## Scene invariants
- [environment, lighting, palette, medium]

## Forbidden drift
- [identity, role, clothing, age, or anatomy changes]
```

Use only verified visual information. An approved character card becomes the primary reference for later scenes.

## Example

Input: `Lock an adult character with shoulder-length dark hair, thin glasses, a green jacket, dark trousers, and white shoes for three classroom scenes.`

Expected sheet: one identity block, exact clothing/accessory list, classroom invariants, and explicit forbidden changes for all three scenes.
