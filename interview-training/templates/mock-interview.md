# Mock Interview

## Format

1. Confirm the target role, level, duration, and focus.
2. Ask one realistic question at a time.
3. Ask the learner to state the test intent and 3–4 answer keywords.
4. Let the learner answer with a conclusion followed by 2–3 points.
5. Ask one narrow follow-up.
6. Give evidence-based feedback and one corrected answer structure.
7. Re-ask a weak question later without hints.

## Example

Input: `Run a 20-minute mock interview for a senior solution product role. Focus on requirements and delivery risks.`

Expected opening: confirm the focus, then ask one scenario question about turning an ambiguous business request into a verifiable solution. Do not show the answer first.
