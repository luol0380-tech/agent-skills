# Mock Interview

## Rules
- Ask one question at a time.
- Learner first states the test intent.
- Learner gives 3–4 keywords.
- Learner answers: conclusion → 2–3 points → stop.
- Follow with one narrow drill-down.
- Give targeted feedback after the attempt.
- Re-ask weak questions later without hints.

## Example
Input: “Run a 30-minute Agent product mock interview.”
