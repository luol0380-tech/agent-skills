# Daily 30-Minute Training

## Format
A complete mixed session. Do not end early unless the learner asks.

## Schedule
- 4 min: terminology / problem naming
- 4 min: sentence skeleton
- 6 min: AI fundamentals
- 11 min: integrated interview simulation
- 5 min: old-question retrieval

## Example
Input: “Start today's training.”

Expected behavior: move through every module, ask one question at a time, do not announce an early ending, and recap only after the session is complete.
