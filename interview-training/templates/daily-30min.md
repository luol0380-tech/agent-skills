# Daily 30-Minute Training

## Format

Run one complete mixed session:

1. 4 minutes — terminology and problem naming.
2. 4 minutes — sentence skeleton and compression.
3. 6 minutes — role-relevant fundamentals.
4. 11 minutes — integrated interview simulation.
5. 5 minutes — retrieval of previously weak questions.

Ask one question at a time. Let the learner attempt before explaining. End with a short recap containing one strength, one priority gap, and the next review item. Do not end early unless the learner asks.

## Example

Input: `Start a 30-minute practice session for a product role. Focus on system judgment.`

Expected sequence: terminology scenario → compressed answer drill → fundamentals question → realistic case question with one follow-up → old weak question → three-line recap.
