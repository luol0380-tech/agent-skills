# Brief Meeting Notes

## Format

```markdown
# [Meeting title]

- Date: [date or TBD]
- Purpose: [one sentence]

## Decisions
- [decision and short reason]

## Action items
- [ ] [verb-led task] — Owner: [role or TBD] — Due: [date or TBD]

## Open questions
- [question and next confirmation step]
```

Use only information supported by the source. Omit an empty section rather than inventing content.

## Example

Input: `The team agreed to test the revised onboarding with ten users next week. The research lead will recruit them by Friday. Pricing was discussed but not decided.`

Output:

```markdown
# Onboarding review

- Date: TBD
- Purpose: Confirm the next onboarding test.

## Decisions
- Test the revised onboarding with ten users next week.

## Action items
- [ ] Recruit ten test users — Owner: Research lead — Due: Friday

## Open questions
- What pricing direction should be tested? Decision remains pending.
```
