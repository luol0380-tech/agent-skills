# Long-Form Article

## Format

```markdown
# [Specific title with tension or outcome]

[Opening: one real scene or problem in 2–4 short paragraphs]

## 01 | What happened
[Verified facts, source material, and the concrete difficulty]

## 02 | What changed the judgment
[Evidence, attempt, result, and limitation]

## 03 | What I learned
[Creator's own conclusion and why it matters]

## A reusable takeaway
- [step, question, or short prompt]
- [boundary or caution]

[Closing that returns to the specific story]
```

Use only the sections the story needs. Put screenshots directly after the claim they support. Keep the creator's style in local private configuration rather than this template.

## Example

Input: `Write about a weekly planning experiment. The source shows that grouping tasks by energy reduced unfinished work, but the result is only from two weeks.`

Expected article: open with one overloaded Monday, explain the energy-based grouping, show the two-week observation, state that the evidence is preliminary, and end with a small method readers can test.
