# AI practice

## Format

### Writing
Begin with a real problem and evidence. Explain actual inputs, process, output, limitations, and human judgment. Include reusable prompts or steps when supported. Show concrete changes in thinking and action; never fabricate results, metrics, or product capabilities.

### Cover
Use the user's approved AI cover reference when available. Without one, use clear mobile-readable hierarchy and restrained technical visual cues. Personal avatars, brand colors, logos and fixed title arrangements come from private configuration only. Print a series number only when explicitly confirmed.

### Body layout
Use `layout_template: ai-practice`: white article, muted blue headings, gray body, restrained pale quote/prompt blocks. Keep readable spacing and captions tied to actual evidence. Preserve approved wording during formatting.

### Ending
Close on the practical outcome, limits or a concrete capability change. Use a fixed series expression, signature or footer only when supplied and approved in private configuration or the current request. Never assume personal branding.

## Example

Fictional brief: use AI to organize a made-up reading list. Show sample input, explain a categorization error, then provide the revised instruction. No claimed real-world result or automatic series number.
