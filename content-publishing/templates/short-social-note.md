# Short Social Note

## Format

```markdown
# [Short title]

[One human-scale tension]

[One concrete scene]

[The unexpected finding]

[What changed in action or judgment]

[One useful takeaway]

[3–6 relevant tags when requested]
```

Write in first person only when the source supports it. Keep paragraphs short. Do not turn a personal story into generic motivational copy or a list of features.

## Example

Input: `Adapt an approved article about improving meeting preparation into a short note. The key scene is arriving with three decision questions instead of a long document.`

Expected note: center the before-and-after meeting scene, explain why three decision questions worked, acknowledge where a full document is still needed, and close with the three-question method.
